humans.js API notes

new Congregation(scene, {places, quality, seed, fill, special}); update(dt, t, beat, beatPhase, energy). Fields: slots [{x, z, yaw, taken}], people, meshes, mood, cpu (ms per update).

mood (0..1, roughly the share of people doing it; people pick moves by taste and age, re-pick every 8 or 16 beats and react to big mood changes with a personal delay): stand, kneel, sleep (sleepy people nod off while seated), sway (side to side, arms loose), clap (front claps; overhead claps grow with energy), raise (hands up V, waving), jump (jumps on the beat, half-time for slower people), mosh (pit only: crowd surges, shoves, stomps, fists), worship (raised palms, slow sway, eyes closed), headbang, peace (turn to a neighbour and shake hands), pump (fist pumping), shuffle (running man), hug (arms around neighbours, the row sways together), sing (mouths move). Energy scales amplitude.

new Clergy(scene, {priest, server, deck = true}): mode idle | orans | talk | elev | hype | dance | breakdance | dj (blended), voice, host, censerPos, priestRoot, serverRoot (move them and a walk cycle blends in), kick(amount), update(dt, t, beat, beatPhase, energy). Breakdance is a 32-beat loop that starts when the mode is set.

URL: mood=sit|stand|clap|jump|hands|mosh|worship|kneel|headbang|sleep|peace|shuffle|hug|pump|mix|sitclap|sitmix, priest=<mode>, cam=back|side|close|face|priest|stage|pclose|dj|bd|bdfront|server|pit|wide|line|lineclose|lineside, t=seconds (freeze), e=energy, light=rave|day, n=people, fill, seed, q=low|mid|high, bpm, lineup=1|back, nopit=1, walk=1, cx cy cz lx ly lz (custom camera).
